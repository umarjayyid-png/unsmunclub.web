01 / ABOUT US

MORE THAN A SIMULATION

Where students
learn to engage
with the world.

UNS Model United Nations Club is a student organization dedicated to developing diplomatic skills, critical thinking, and global awareness through Model United Nations and beyond.

Through academic discussions, professional development, delegation training, and conference experiences, we create an environment where members can research global issues, speak with confidence, negotiate thoughtfully, and collaborate across perspectives.
